Services

OptiPEC supports public and private organizations in structuring, securing and improving the performance of their projects through governance, project delivery and energy expertise.

We operate as a strategic and operational partner, capable of supporting teams directly on-site when required.

Governance, PMO, strategic advisory & optimization

We help organizations structure their Project Management Offices (PMOs), strengthen governance and align projects with business strategy.

  • PMO design, setup and optimization
  • Definition of roles, committees and decision-making frameworks
  • Strategic alignment of projects and portfolio roadmap
  • Performance dashboards and KPIs (cost, schedule, risks, benefits)
  • Organizational diagnostics and targeted improvement plans
  • Change management and executive advisory
  • AI & digital transformation support integrated into governance, business needs and real operational environments

Project delivery & performance – energy, infrastructure & transformation

OptiPEC helps organizations structure, secure and enhance the delivery of complex initiatives by bringing visibility, control and measurable performance to project execution.

Our approach focuses on reducing risks, improving coordination and ensuring that projects are not only delivered, but perform.

  • End-to-end project structuring and delivery oversight (scope, schedule, budget, risk)
  • Stakeholder coordination (municipalities, contractors, suppliers, consultants)
  • Performance monitoring and executive reporting (KPIs, governance, EVM)
  • Risk, contract and change management across the project lifecycle
  • Support for complex energy and infrastructure projects
  • Lessons learned, standardization and continuous improvement

Energy solutions – solar, thermal & efficiency

We support organizations in evaluating, optimizing and implementing energy solutions to improve performance and support strategic decision-making.

  • Technical studies and feasibility assessments (solar, thermal, storage, hybrid)
  • Energy audits and performance evaluations for buildings and sites
  • Techno-economic analysis and ROI assessment (CAPEX/OPEX, payback, NPV)
  • Energy-efficiency scenarios and operating cost reduction strategies
  • Integration of solar systems, battery storage and hybrid energy solutions
  • Strategic recommendations and decision support for leadership teams

Case Studies

Dynamic Signage — Governance & Quality

Supported a multi-stakeholder digital signage rollout where coordination across vendors, technical teams and decision-makers was creating delays and inconsistent quality control. Implemented a governance framework with clear roles, review checkpoints and risk tracking to bring structure to execution.

Result: Improved coordination across stakeholder groups and reduced operational risk throughout delivery.

Solar PV — Technical Validation

Conducted technical validation for a solar PV installation, including system sizing review, compliance verification and performance benchmarking against design targets. Identified sizing and configuration adjustments before commissioning to avoid costly rework.

Result: Improved system reliability and gave the client clear, data-backed confidence in their investment decision.

Process Optimization

Mapped existing operational processes to identify bottlenecks and inconsistent tracking practices. Structured a set of KPIs aligned with business priorities and introduced a continuous improvement cycle for ongoing monitoring.

Result: Increased operational efficiency and gave leadership clearer visibility into performance.
